Read the word. Find its rhythm.

Russian on the front, English on the back. The accent mark (а́) shows stress; ё is always stressed. Related noun/verb forms share a card and keep their roles in the translation. Vocabulary is a curated beginner A1/A2 selection; labels are study guides, not a complete official exam list.

A few keys to keep you moving.
